About

The Bureau for Research and Economic Analysis of Development (BREAD) organizes annual conferences outside the United States and Europe to generate positive spillovers for the development economics community in the host region. These conferences bring together leading researchers and policymakers to advance rigorous evidence on global development.

Mexico City offers a natural hub for Latin America and the Caribbean (LAC), enabling knowledge spillovers to regional scholars, universities, and policy institutions. The 2026 edition will feature a dedicated mentoring component for early-career researchers based in LAC, connecting them with senior economists from the global development economics community.

The conference format combines full-length research presentations with shorter early-career sessions and a structured mentoring component. Discussions are led by economists with deep expertise in the presented work.

Learn more about BREAD and its mission at ibread.org.

Call for Papers

We invite submissions of original research on any topic within development economics. Empirical, theoretical, and policy-relevant work are all welcome.

Eligibility

All researchers are eligible to submit. Priority will be given to scholars based in Latin America or the Caribbean (LAC) and to early-career researchers.

Session Types

  • Full-length presentations — standard research presentations with discussants, open to all eligible researchers.
  • Early-career sessions — shorter presentations designed for PhD students and junior faculty, with structured feedback from senior economists.
